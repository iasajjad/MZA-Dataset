This device is smart energy meter of ACREL company ADL400N.
It is basically a three channel device. Each channel is connected with a circuit, the description of circuit is as follow;
Cicuit A:
	Kitchen Circuit connected load: Fridge (220W) rating, Micorwave (Rating of 2000W), Lightning load (approximatley 50W) and Electric heater (1KW).
Circuit B
	Electric iron (1KW) and Lighning+Fan load (nearly 300W).
Circuit C:
	Water Pump (1KW) and Lighning (nearly 50W)
